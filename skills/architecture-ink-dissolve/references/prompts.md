# Prompts de generación

Herramienta utilizada: image_gen integrado. Cada llamada produce un cartel independiente. Las comparaciones se montan después colocando los archivos completos, sin volver a generar la fotografía.

## 01 · Memoria — primera generación

```text
Use case: style-transfer.
Create ONE finished 3:4 portrait fine-art architectural poster from the attached real source photograph. The photograph is the ONLY source of the architecture: Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests, Temple of Heaven, exactly three circular blue tiled roof tiers and its gold finial. Keep the actual low-angle perspective, curved eave silhouettes, blue-green-gold painted bracket details, red base doors, and white stone terrace. Extract the building with a SMALL amount of terrace immediately below it; remove sky, all clouds and the long foreground stairs. Do not recreate a different frontal view. Retain photographic color/materials from the source on the RIGHT side.

Composition: smooth warm ivory matte paper, very subtle fine fibers, no vintage stains. Center the extracted building on the exact vertical middle, slightly low, occupying about 62% of canvas width and 43% of canvas height; allow ample clean paper above, side margins, and below. One physical continuous building, no mirrored copy: its vertical center divides it precisely 50/50 into left ink / right photo. Matching roofs, brackets, doors and terrace must join with zero displacement at the center seam.

RIGHT HALF: faithful detailed color photography, original muted blue/green/gold/stone colors and natural shadows, crisp outline and clean base.
LEFT HALF: convert architecture into delicate BLACK AND GRAY DRY-INK RUBBING on paper, etched tile lines, fine horizontal rubbing, broken dry-brush white holes, not a grayscale photograph. Near center preserve architectural legibility; moving toward the left, eaves and brackets progressively unravel into broken linework, then thin ink remnants, then sparse faint traces. The paper must appear through INTERNAL lattice/bracket details as well as exterior contour. Fragmentation follows the original curved eaves and structural beams horizontally leftward. Keep all residual marks thin, light and small, preserving roof direction. No giant black masses, smoke, dust cloud, exploding chunks, thick splashes, random dot overlay or glowing particles.
A hairline pale-gray interrupted vertical axis extends briefly above and below the building into blank paper, extremely subtle, never a thick divider.
Below the building just LEFT of the axis add the exact small Spanish handwritten inscription "Memoria" in airy light gray-black brush lettering. Under it one tiny plain cinnabar rectangle containing only a simple abstract pale line, no glyphs. No Chinese, Japanese or English text. No additional headline, frame, diagrams, collages, original/result labels, UI, watermark, sun, moon or circular ornament.
The final should feel quiet, spacious and architecturally precise. The dissolving ink is structural and airy rather than explosive.
```

## 02 · Huella dorada — generación elegida

```text
Use case: style-transfer. Create ONE standalone 3:4 portrait fine art architectural poster.

Select and isolate ONLY the golden main pavilion in image1 with its small stone foundation. Remove ALL forest, pine trees, mountains, sky, pond, reflections and distant people. Preserve the source's exact existing viewpoint and building proportions: three stories, TWO dark curved broad main roof tiers, a small golden phoenix finial, TWO gold upper stories with railings, the dark wood/white plaster ground story, asymmetric corner perspective. Keep the existing visible doors, railings and window divisions; do not add a third main roof, symmetrize or mirror.
COMPOSITION IS SPACIOUS: smooth warm ivory matte paper. Picture a centered 3:4 page with the building confined to x=21%–79%, y=39%–76% of the page; architecture width58% of page and height37%, preserving its source proportions. Leave the upper third of page completely EMPTY ivory. Tiny inscription sits below around y80%. Do NOT enlarge subject to fill page.
A vertical seam at x50% divides this SAME uninterrupted building into equal left monochrome ink and right true-color photographic surface. The existing perspective, eaves and railings connect accurately at the seam.
RIGHT side: faithful golden yellow upper walls and rails, dark gray-brown roofing, brown wood/white ground story and natural source shadows.
LEFT side: black/gray dry ink rubbing, fine engraved structural outlines, horizontal drybrush and broken white voids. Near center clear roof/windows/rails; toward outer left they progressively break into thin discontinuous lines, small residual ink films and sparse fading directional marks. Paper holes open INSIDE the architecture as well as its silhouette; dissolution flows along eaves, railings and beam directions. All left building parts including phoenix must be monochrome. The mark quality is fine, airy and light. No dense black cloud or chunky flying masonry, no explosion, smoke, random dots over grayscale photo or glowing particles.
One barely visible fine interrupted gray vertical axis x50% extends briefly above and below building, from y29% to y87%; never a strong divider.
Under the building just left of center, exact small Spanish inscription "Huella dorada" in airy gray-black handwriting, not a title. Below it tiny plain cinnabar rectangle with only one pale abstract line, no glyphs. All other areas stay blank.
No Chinese/Japanese/English text, added words, UI, watermark, picture frame, border, split panels, source photo inset, labels, sun, moon, ornament circles or extra buildings. Refined architectural identity, quiet negative space.
```

## 03 · Ecos del tiempo — primera generación

```text
Use case: style-transfer. Create one finished 3:4 portrait architectural art poster.
Input 1 is the ONLY subject source: the red Shitennoji pagoda photograph in Osaka. Input 2 is STYLE AND SPACING REFERENCE ONLY, the previous finished Memoria poster; do not reuse its building.
Extract the pagoda only, removing sky/clouds, people and adjacent buildings/trees. Preserve the actual oblique low-angle view, EXACTLY FIVE broad roof tiers, the tall gold spire with ring details, red structural beams, ivory soffits, green railings and low stone base. Do not invent levels, mirror the building or substitute frontal symmetry. No broad foreground stairs or adjacent roofs.
Use smooth warm ivory near-blank matte paper. The complete pagoda spans about 55% canvas width and 50% height, centered x50%, center y56%; preserve its slender source proportions. Upper paper stays empty and spacious.
A precise vertical seam at x50% divides ONE continuous pagoda. Every roofline, beam and platform aligns across the seam at the original perspective.
The RIGHT half is faithful richly detailed photographic color in the source red/ivory/green/gold palette, naturally lit. All architecture LEFT of center, including spire, is entirely neutral black/gray dry-ink rubbing: fine etched lines, horizontal grain, drybrush white breaks. Moving outward LEFT, architectural details gradually fragment along the roof/beam directions into thinner broken lines, then wispy ink traces and blank paper. Open paper holes inside the lattice/details as well as the exterior. Keep the five roof silhouettes legible near the seam, increasing structural loss only outward. Fine, light, restrained marks, not dense black cloudy masses, splatter, exploding blocks or a monochrome photo with random dots.
Subtle very fine interrupted gray central axis extends briefly above and below the pagoda, no thick dividing line.
Under the building to the left of the axis, exact tiny airy handwritten Spanish inscription: "Ecos del tiempo". A very small plain cinnabar-red rectangular seal below it, containing only an abstract pale line, no characters.
No Chinese/Japanese/English or other text, labels, headline, frames, collage, UI, watermark, sun/moon, circles, fog, glow, particles or added shadows. Maintain spaciousness and architecture identity.
```
