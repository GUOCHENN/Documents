# Prompts utilizados

Herramienta: image_gen integrada. La captura aportada por el usuario solo se usó para comunicar el aspecto de una miniatura sobre base; nunca se incorporó a las salidas ni al paquete.

## 1

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: studio render of an original miniature tabletop diorama derived from a real photograph.
Input images: Image 1 is the SUBJECT reference, Misky's photograph of a handled wicker basket filled with red-yellow apples. Image 2 is a STYLE/FORMAT reference screenshot ONLY: learn from its LOWER model image the complete rounded display base, elevated camera angle, natural miniature material, soft studio light and uncluttered cream setting. Never reproduce the screenshot, its upper source photo, face, phone interface, logos, Chinese text or social-media controls.
Primary request: turn the actual apple basket in Image 1 into a finely crafted small collectible diorama. Preserve the basket's oval body, single tall loop handle, overflowing round red-yellow apples and their clustered arrangement. Reduce the many apples to about ten distinct fruit forms with subtle variation, keeping the pale apple near the handle and the red apples at the front as recognizable anchors. Do not turn the basket into the water basin from Image 2.
Build a complete rounded rectangular pale stone/clay display plinth beneath the basket, with a tiny patch of ground and just three or four source-derived autumn leaves at one side. Show the entire plinth perimeter and its visible thickness. Basket and apples sit firmly on the base, not floating. Wicker weave becomes a few broad carefully modeled bands, not photoreal tiny fibers. Apples retain small stem dimples, restrained red-yellow color transitions and tactile hand-painted detail. No added characters, animals or unrelated props.
Camera: elevated three-quarter orthographic-like product view, roughly 35 degrees downward, centred slightly below middle, the tall handle entirely visible with ample clear margin. Portrait 3:4 image. Warm cream seamless background, no horizon, no real environment, no frame. Whole object uses about 65 percent of image height and 75 percent of width.
Materials: high-quality matte painted resin and soft clay, gently rounded but not melted, grounded natural harvest colors with muted red, ochre and warm straw. Fine material realism without heavy photographic surface noise. Soft large-source light from upper left, gentle contact shadow beneath the base. Crisp whole-object focus; no extreme tilt-shift blur. Do not make it flat illustration, glossy candy plastic, paper cutout or a real full-size basket on a table. No text, labels, logos, signatures or watermark. Deliver just the finished single diorama.
```
